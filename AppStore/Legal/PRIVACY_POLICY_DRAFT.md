## 1. Information we collect

### Account information
We collect information needed to create and maintain your account, including your email address, first name, authentication identifiers and records showing your acceptance of our legal terms.

### Dog and owner profiles
We collect information you submit about yourself and your dogs, including names, photos, breed, sex, date of birth or approximate age, location area, descriptions, connection purposes, pedigree availability and health-related details.

### Vaccination information
We collect vaccination names and dates and, when you choose to provide them, clinic information and document images. This information is supplied by users and is not independently verified by Hussle unless expressly stated.

### Location
With your permission, Hussle processes location information to show dogs within a selected radius. We display only approximate distance or a broad area to other users, not your precise coordinates. You may choose a city manually instead of granting location access.

### Matches and communications
We collect likes, passes, matches, messages, blocks, reports and moderation records required to operate and protect the service.

### Device and technical information
We may collect device push tokens, app version, operating-system version, security events, diagnostic data and limited usage events needed to maintain and improve the service.

### Support information
We collect the information you include when you contact support or submit a report.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:
- provide accounts, profiles, discovery, matching and messaging;
- calculate approximate distance and rank relevant profiles;
- upload, display and moderate user content;
- send service notifications when enabled;
- investigate reports, enforce rules and protect users and animals;
- prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access;
- provide support and respond to legal requests;
- maintain, test and improve Hussle.

## 3. Legal bases

Where applicable, we process information because it is necessary to provide the service you request, because you have consented, because we have legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or because processing is required by law.

## 4. How information is shared

### With other Hussle users
Other users may see your dog profiles, optional owner profile, broad location area, approximate distance and information you choose to make visible. Matches can exchange messages. We do not display your account email or exact coordinates.

### Service providers
We use service providers for hosting, authentication, storage, email delivery, notifications, moderation, diagnostics and support. They may process information only to provide contracted services to us.

### Safety and legal disclosures
We may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or prevent harm to people or animals.

### Business transfers
If Hussle is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization or sale of assets, information may be transferred subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell personal information or use Hussle data for cross-company targeted advertising in version 1.0.

## 5. Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account operation, security, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Final production retention periods must be inserted here.

When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify associated information unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or the protection of other users. Reports and moderation evidence may be retained for a limited period where necessary.

## 6. Account deletion and user choices

You can edit profile information, adjust discovery and notification settings, block users, and initiate account deletion within Hussle:

Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account

You can control location, photo and notification permissions through iOS Settings. Declining a permission may limit the related feature; manual city selection is available for discovery.

## 7. Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. These include encrypted transport, access controls, private storage, server-side authorization rules and moderation logging. No service can guarantee absolute security.
