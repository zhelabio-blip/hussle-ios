# Hussle Community Guidelines

Hussle is designed for respectful dog owners and responsible connections.

## Be accurate
Use current photos and provide truthful information about the dog’s identity, breed, age, sex, ownership, vaccination records, health tests and purpose.

## Put animal welfare first
Do not promote harmful, coercive, medically unsuitable, underage or illegal breeding. Never use Hussle to facilitate abuse, neglect, fighting or exploitation.

## Treat people respectfully
Harassment, threats, hate, stalking, sexual content involving people, impersonation and disclosure of private information are prohibited.

## Avoid scams and pressure
Do not request urgent payments, misrepresent documents, advertise prohibited sales or send repetitive commercial messages.

## Keep profiles appropriate
Upload clear dog-related photos. Do not upload graphic, sexual, violent, hateful, stolen or text-heavy promotional content.

## Protect privacy
Do not publish home addresses, identity documents, private medical documents or another person’s contact information in public profile fields.

## Report concerns
Use Report for suspected animal harm, scams, fake profiles, threats, underage users or inappropriate content. Use Block when you do not want further interaction.

Hussle may remove content, limit visibility, pause profiles, suspend accounts or permanently ban users who violate these rules.
