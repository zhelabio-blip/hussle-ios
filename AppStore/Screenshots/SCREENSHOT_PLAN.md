# Hussle App Store Screenshot Plan

Apple allows one to ten screenshots. Use six portrait screenshots for the initial listing. Capture real UI from the final Release build; do not use features or text absent from the submitted app.

## Recommended master size
Use an accepted 6.9-inch portrait size, such as 1320 × 2868 px or another currently accepted size shown in App Store Connect at upload time.

## Visual rules
- Preserve the approved light minimalist interface.
- Use large dog photography and very little overlay text.
- Keep headlines outside critical UI regions.
- Do not describe Hussle as “Tinder for dogs” in storefront assets.
- Do not use unverified claims such as “verified health records.”
- Do not show exact addresses or precise map pins.
- Ensure all screenshots match the English interface.
