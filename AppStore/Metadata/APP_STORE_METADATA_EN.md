## Description
Hussle helps dog owners discover compatible dogs nearby through a simple, focused experience.

Create a profile for your dog, add photos and vaccination information, choose what you are looking for, and browse nearby profiles. Hussle prioritizes dogs that match your dog’s breed and connection goals while keeping exact locations private.

Use Hussle to find:

• Suitable dogs for responsible breeding discussions
• Local companions for walks
• New friends for socialization

When two owners both express interest, Hussle creates a match and opens a private conversation.

Key features:

• Simple swipe-based discovery
• Search by distance, breed, sex, age and purpose
• Dog profiles with vaccination dates and optional health details
• Mutual matches before messaging
• Private in-app chat
• Approximate distance without exposing exact location
• Reporting, blocking and unmatching tools
• In-app account deletion

Responsible breeding matters

Hussle helps owners connect but does not certify medical suitability, pedigree, fertility or genetic compatibility. Always consult a licensed veterinarian and review appropriate health tests, vaccination records and ownership documents before arranging breeding.

Hussle is intended for users aged 18 and older.
