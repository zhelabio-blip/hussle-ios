# Hussle v0.12 — App Store Readiness Package

This version adds the complete English storefront and policy drafting package under `AppStore/`:

- metadata and description;
- privacy-disclosure working map;
- Privacy Policy draft;
- Terms of Use draft;
- Community Guidelines;
- App Review notes;
- screenshot plan;
- release-submission checklist.

## Important
The legal documents are implementation drafts, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Complete all placeholders and obtain appropriate legal review before public launch.

The first real Xcode compilation remains a required gate. As of July 2026, Apple requires App Store uploads to be built with Xcode 26 or later using the iOS 26 SDK or later.
